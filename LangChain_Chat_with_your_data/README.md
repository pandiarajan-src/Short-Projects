# LangChain: Chat with your Data

Worked through the [deeplearning.ai](https://www.deeplearning.ai) short course ["LangChain: Chat with Your Data"](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/langchain-chat-with-your-data/). One notebook per lesson, learning to build a chatbot that answers questions about your own documents.

## Lessons

- `01_document_loading.ipynb` — loading documents
- `02_document_splitting.ipynb` — splitting documents
- `03_vectorstores_and_embeddings.ipynb` — vectorstores and embeddings
- `04_retrieval.ipynb` — retrieval
- `05_question_answering.ipynb` — question answering
- `06_chat.ipynb` — the chat interface

Supporting folders: `docs/` (sample documents), `img/` and `snaps/`.
